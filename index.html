01 — About

Who I Am

I'm a Finance major at Tulane University's A.B. Freeman School of Business, graduating May 2027, with a minor in Entrepreneurial Business and a 3.7 GPA. Based in Bethesda, MD, I'm passionate about deal-making, capital markets, and the intersection of technology and finance.
